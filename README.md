## Read this before you pay: the refund policy

RackNerd does **not** offer a money-back guarantee. Their terms state that refunds are handled case by case, and that "by default no refunds are provided once payment is received." Their knowledge base is blunter still: no refunds, no money-back period. If you cancel an annual plan early, don't expect the unused months back. There's also a documented sting for disputes — filing a credit card chargeback can trigger an administrative fee between $125 and $250 and possibly a suspended account.

This isn't a dealbreaker given the prices, but it should shape how you order:

- **Unsure about the service?** Start with the monthly plan. $5.59 is a cheap way to find out it's wrong for you.
- **Confident?** Take the annual rate and save the ~$56.
- **Never** pay with a payment method you'd be tempted to chargeback — use PayPal or a card you trust, and direct problems to their support first. Support runs 24/7 with a published average response time around 10 minutes, which is a better path than a dispute either way.

## How ordering works, and where people trip up

The whole flow takes a few minutes. Here's the sequence, based on RackNerd's own order walkthrough:

1. Pick a plan on the shared hosting page and click Order Now.
2. **Choose your domain step carefully.** You can register a new domain, transfer one, or just enter an existing domain and point its nameservers later. Known glitch: if the domain you type is already attached to your RackNerd account, the form may throw an error — switching to a different extension or domain name gets you past it.
3. Select your billing cycle and **datacenter location**: Los Angeles, New York City, Germany (Frankfurt), or France (Strasbourg) at no extra cost. **Singapore adds $8/year.** Pick wherever your visitors are; NYC went live in late 2025 and is the natural pick for East Coast and European audiences.
4. Checkout. RackNerd accepts PayPal, credit cards, cryptocurrency, and bank wire.
5. Activation. The marketing pages say plans are "immediately activated"; the official blog says typically within 5–15 minutes. Either way, you're not waiting days. Your confirmation email contains your cPanel login details, and everything is managed from the RackNerd client area.

Free migration is one of the better perks here, but check the fine print: it applies when your current host also uses **cPanel**. RackNerd handles a cPanel-to-cPanel move for free — you open a support ticket with your old host's login details after ordering. Coming from a non-cPanel host (some custom-panel budget hosts, or a self-managed VPS), you'd be moving files and databases yourself.

## Honest limitations

A few things don't show up in the marketing copy:

- **Domain counts are hard limits.** The 30 GB plan hosts 2 domains, the 85 GB plan 10. The official blog has at times described the entry plan as "unlimited domains," but the current promo pages and order system say 2 — trust the order form you're actually buying from. If your project has any chance of growing past a couple of sites, the 85 GB tier removes that ceiling for $14.50 more per year.
- **No refunds**, as covered above. This is the single most important policy to internalize before a multi-year prepaid order.
- **Reselling is a different product.** The 200 GB shared plan allows unlimited add-on domains, but if you're genuinely reselling hosting to clients, RackNerd's separate reseller line ($34/$64/$94 per year for 3/8/12 cPanel accounts) includes WHM, which gives each client an independent cPanel login. The shared plans don't.
- Resource quotas like inode limits, CPU seconds, or email account counts aren't published in the plan listings — if a specific quota matters to your project, ask their support before ordering rather than assuming.
